Skill 2

A Skill 3 hiding in a Skill 2.

A familiar 10 slash model, reimagined. Each successful kill recycles up to 10% of the maximum slashes back into the skill, keeping you going from Dusk to Dawn.

Mobility

Slash. Finish. Reposition.

When the slashes end, Chen will redeploy to the final enemy struck within range. Paired with up to 60% improved survivability and a 300% attack increase after completing her slashes, Skill 2 opens up entirely new approaches to map positioning.

Skill 3

Heavenly Sigh.

Skill 3 introduces HP% scaling, adapting to enemies and the task at hand. With over 60 hits and over 480k damage per skill, it delivers an unprecendented level of output.
